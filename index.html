Our Story

Hello, I am Rajesh Sharma.

In 1994, my father Late Shri Mohan Lal Sharma set up a single kadhai outside this very shop with a loan from my chacha. I was twelve and my job was to wash the trays and run laddoos to the neighbours.

Thirty years later I still wake at 4 AM to light the stove. The recipes have not changed. My mother's motichoor method is still our measure, and the boondi is still hand-poured.

Many of our customers have been coming for three generations. That is the reason we never cut corners with ghee or with khoya.

  • Only pure desi ghee, never vanaspati
  • Khoya and paneer made in-house every day
  • Fresh batches morning and evening
  • Custom boxes with a free taste before you order
  • Honest weight and honest price, every time
